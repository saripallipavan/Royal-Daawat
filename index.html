Our Story

ROYAL DAAWAT

Greetings, food enthusiasts! I'm a journeyer of flavors, a connoisseur of spices, and the heart behind Royal Daawat. The story begins in the vibrant lands where my taste buds were first introduced to the mesmerizing world of Indian cuisine.

My journey took me to the bustling streets, where the fragrant curries, the sizzling tandoori, and the symphony of spices ignited a passion within me that could not be ignored. Armed with my passion and a heart full of dreams, I embarked on an adventure – Royal Daawat cuisine.

This restaurant isn't just a place to eat; it's an experience designed to transport your taste buds across the diverse landscapes of India.
